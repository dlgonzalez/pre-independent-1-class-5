Writing: A Blog Post

A good survival story needs more than just facts. It needs emotion, opinion, and a clear structure. Let's learn how to write a compelling blog post.

Step 1: Analyze the Model

An Amazing Story

Hi, guys! I want to share with you an amazing story of survival. Some of you might know about it already. In 2018, 12 teenagers and their football coach went into caves in Thailand. It started to rain and they couldn't get out because of the water. They were in there for nine days without any food or light. They drank water from the cave walls!

Divers eventually found them after a long search, but they still had a problem. The water made it very hard to get the boys out, and most of them couldn't swim. And it was the start of the rainy season, so they didn't have a lot of time. Finally, the divers succeeded in getting all the boys out, but it wasn't easy.

I hate the idea of being stuck in a dark cave! I'd be very scared. You need to be calm and independent to survive a situation like that. I think the boys were really brave, and so were the divers who rescued them. They had to be creative to find a solution to the problem, but they did it. If you are curious, search online and read the whole story.

Based on the text, is the statement True or False?

1. The writer speaks directly to the readers to create a friendly style.
2. The writer gives his/her personal reaction AND THEN describes what happened.
3. The writer explains what problems different people faced.
4. The writer imagines himself/herself in the survival situation.
5. The writer gives an opinion of the people in the situation.

Step 2: Draft Your Post

Instructions

  • Choose a famous survival story (Juliane Koepcke, The Andes Flight, Aron Ralston) OR a personal story.
  • Paragraph 1: Speak directly to the reader. What happened? Who? When? What was the biggest problem?
  • Paragraph 2: How did they survive? (Use grammar: managed to, succeeded in, was able to).
  • Paragraph 3: What is your reaction? (Use character adjectives: brave, resilient, resourceful).
